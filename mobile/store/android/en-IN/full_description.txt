FinderB is a local business directory for India. Search for shops, service providers, doctors, tutors, salons, restaurants, real estate agents, contractors and more in your city, see their photos and customer reviews, and contact them in one tap.

FIND BUSINESSES NEAR YOU
• Browse hundreds of categories, from home services and repairs to health, education, food and shopping
• See listings sorted by distance from your current location, or pick any city in India
• Search by name, service or need, by typing or with voice search
• Open full business profiles with photos, opening hours, address, map location and services offered

CONTACT IN ONE TAP
• Call a business or message it on WhatsApp straight from its profile
• Send an enquiry once and nearby businesses in that category can respond
• Save favourite businesses so you can find them again quickly

HONEST REVIEWS
• Read ratings and reviews from other customers
• Share your own experience after you visit a business
• Businesses can reply to reviews publicly

MORE FOR YOUR CITY
• Local news in English and Hindi
• Job openings posted by local businesses
• Tourist places, city guides and blog articles
• Notifications for updates you care about

FOR BUSINESS OWNERS
List your business on FinderB for free and manage it from the app:
• Edit your profile, photos, hours, services and contact details
• Get new customer leads and enquiries, with alerts on your phone
• Reply to reviews and track how many people viewed your listing
• Post job openings
• Upgrade to a paid plan for extra visibility

Your privacy matters. FinderB uses your location only to show nearby results, and you can delete your account at any time from the app.
